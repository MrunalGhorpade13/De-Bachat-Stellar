# Community Contribution Guide

To complete your **Black Belt Level 6** submission, you need to share De-Bachat with the world. Below are step-by-step instructions and templates for your post.

## 📱 Platform Options
While the official guidelines suggest **Twitter (X)**, many users also use **LinkedIn** or **Discord**. Choose the one where you have the most reach!

---

## 🐦 Option 1: Twitter (X) - Recommended
**Step-by-Step:**
1. Open your [Metrics Dashboard](http://localhost:3000/dashboard) and take a screenshot.
2. Go to [Twitter.com](https://twitter.com).
3. Draft a post using the template below:
   > 🚀 Excited to share my Black Belt project for the Stellar Soroban dApp Challenge: **De-Bachat**!
   > 
   > De-Bachat is a decentralized ROSCA (chit fund) dApp built on @StellarOrg Soroban. 
   > ⚡ Features: Gasless UX (Fee Bump), Multi-wallet support, & a live indexing dashboard!
   > 
   > Check out my metrics: [Attach Screenshot]
   > #Stellar #Soroban #Web3 #BuildOnStellar

4. **Copy the link** to your post once published.
5. Paste the link into your `README.md` under the "Community Contribution" section.
